Research profile

My current work is rooted in mechanics of interfaces and durability. For doctoral research, I am especially interested in extending these methods toward metastructures, metamaterials, biomechanics, and medical-device mechanics.

Current focus

Bolted-joint mechanics

Nonlinear hysteresis, interface evolution, preload control, thread-load distribution, stiffness, fatigue behavior, and manufacturing-error effects.

Current focus

Tribology & surface engineering

Friction and wear, high-temperature dry-film degradation, silver-coating lifetime prediction, residual thickness, and post-failure morphology.

PhD direction

Metastructures & biomechanics

Interest in mechanics-driven design for metamaterials, biomechanical systems, and medical devices, building on interface mechanics and computational modeling.
