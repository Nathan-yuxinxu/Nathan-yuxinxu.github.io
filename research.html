Tribological Degradation Mechanisms and Lifetime Prediction of Silver Coatings Modulated by Substrate Materials

Developed a residual-thickness-decay framework for silver-coating life prediction, incorporating substrate roughness, hardness, elastic modulus, and normal load to relate coating wear rate to metallic contact conditions.

  • Defined failure life as sliding distance to a critical residual coating thickness.
  • Validated the framework using friction curves, residual-thickness measurements, and post-failure surface morphology.
  • Outcome: first-author manuscript in preparation.

Interfacial Degradation and Anomalous Disassembly Torque Increase of High-Temperature Dry-Film Coated Threaded Connections

Developed a temperature–coating condition–interface wear–breakaway torque–bolt stress framework using asperity contact theory, adhesive-wear mechanisms, and the Tresca maximum shear stress criterion.

  • Conducted four controlled bolt–self-locking-nut tests at 400 °C and after further heating to 700 °C.
  • Observed peak breakaway torque reaching 4.62× the room-temperature value.
  • Microscopy linked adhesive wear and material transfer in the locking region to the torque increase.

Study on Nonlinear Hysteresis Behavior and Evolution of Bolted Joint Interface

Built a parametric ANSYS Workbench model of a single-bolt lap joint incorporating tightening, thread contact, bearing-surface nonparallelism, and roughness.

  • Evaluated 16 combinations of tightening torque and cyclic tangential displacement.
  • Quantified hysteresis, equivalent stiffness, and loss factor.
  • Increasing bearing-surface nonparallelism from 0.5° to 1° raised the average change rate of equivalent stiffness from 11.28% to 19.32% and loss factor from 9.86% to 12.32%.
Key ContributorApr. 2025 – Sep. 2025

Development and Research on Advanced Threaded Connection Technology

Developed theoretical and finite-element models of bolted-joint deformation and investigated preload, thread pitch, structural parameters, and material properties.

  • Derived an equal-stress optimal pitch distribution at 568.65 kN preload.
  • Finite-element validation reduced maximum thread stress by 13.83% and increased thread stiffness by 8.9%.
  • Torque-angle tightening reduced preload dispersion from 19.29% to 10.3% versus torque control.
  • Findings supported a wind-turbine blade-root bolting system and successful project acceptance.
